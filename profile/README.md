## Who We Are

**Jipay** is an Algeria-based technology initiative focused on modern financial experiences and ambitious digital products. We are building a digital payment ecosystem designed to make transactions more accessible, intuitive, and transparent—while remaining open to software engineering projects beyond fintech.

From a new product idea to a complex business platform, our ambition is to turn real-world challenges into reliable, thoughtfully engineered software.

## Jipay Fintech: What We're Building

Our payment ecosystem is being developed around practical everyday needs for individuals, merchants, and businesses.

| Solution | What it enables |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Digital wallets** | A mobile-first experience for managing payment accounts and activity. |
| **Instant transfers** | Send money to other users using simple identifiers. |
| **QR-code payments** | Scan and pay, or accept customer payments through merchant QR codes. |
| **Payment links** | Share a link to request and collect payments remotely. |
| **In-person payment codes** | Convenient face-to-face payments using time-limited codes. |
| **Scheduled transfers** | Plan payments for a future date. |
| **Merchant & business tools** | Business payment workflows, reporting, and transaction management. |
| **Payment integrations** | APIs and integration possibilities for business applications. |

> **Product status:** Jipay's payment features are part of its product roadmap and early-access offering. Visit [jipay.dev](https://jipay.dev/) for current availability and official details.
